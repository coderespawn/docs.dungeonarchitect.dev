Foundry is the industrial sci-fi theme that ships with the plugin.   This video series builds it from scratch,
and it is the most complete worked example of the theming techniques described in this section - selection
rules, probability, pattern matching and the interactive editor mode all show up in a real theme rather than in
isolation

The finished theme is at `Dungeon Architect Content > Showcase > Samples > Themes > Foundry`, so you can open it
and compare against your own as you follow along
